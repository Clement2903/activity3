ABOUT ME

A little about my journey

I am a dedicated 3rd-year Computer Science student at Mulungushi University, based in Solwezi, with a strong passion for software engineering, system architecture, and modern web development. Transitioning into computer science has fueled my drive to solve complex problems through clean code and intuitive user interface design. Beyond academic coursework in digital design, object-oriented programming, and statistics, I am constantly exploring full stack web technologies and hands on software design patterns. My goal is to leverage my computational skills and practical mindset to engineer scalable applications, contribute to impactful open source projects, and build a fulfilling career in technology.
